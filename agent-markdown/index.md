# HM Pinnacle Consulting

People operations consulting, HR infrastructure, and leadership systems for manufacturing, aerospace, construction, and industrial organizations.

HM Pinnacle helps operational teams stabilize the people side of growth: leadership behavior, hiring discipline, onboarding, accountability, workforce retention, and cross-border HR infrastructure.

## Primary Services

- Workforce stability systems for manufacturers and industrial operators.
- Modern HR infrastructure for growing teams.
- Leadership operating systems for frontline and executive alignment.
- Cross-border HR support for United States and Canada operations.
- Project-based implementation for specific people-system gaps.
- Consulting engagements for CEOs, founders, and operational leaders.

## Who HM Pinnacle Supports

HM Pinnacle most often supports manufacturing, aerospace, construction, and other operationally intensive organizations where frontline leadership, safety, compliance, retention, and workforce stability affect output.

Typical fit:

- Growth-stage operators with roughly 25 to 500 employees.
- Multi-site or cross-border teams.
- Organizations preparing for expansion, acquisition, leadership transition, or workforce redesign.
- Leaders who need practical people systems, not generic HR advice.
